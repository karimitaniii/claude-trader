# Things I don't like

User feedback to retrieve when asked for the "I don't like" list. This is the master list (the copy in Cursor's
app folder is older and incomplete).

- 2026-11-26: Dislikes the strategy's starting allocation of equal weights across eight stocks (12.5% each before risk limits). Wants this recorded for later discussion. No replacement sizing rule chosen and no strategy change authorized by this note.
- 2026-11-26: Dislikes having to open the laptop and manually run the strategies every trading day during US market hours. Wants operation to continue while away, on holiday, or with the laptop off. Scheduling only on the Mac does not meet this preference. Hosting and automation options are still being discussed; nothing deployed or scheduled.
- 2026-11-26: Dislikes that neither strategy currently uses AI or an LLM. Aware that an LLM layer is already a future roadmap idea; wants this preference explicitly recorded for future implementation.
- 2026-11-26: Dislikes that company earnings do not inform trading decisions. Wants earnings information considered as a future feature; the existing earnings research script does not satisfy this preference.
- 2026-11-26: Dislikes that the strategies do not directly react to news, such as a CEO being fired. Wants news inputs and responses considered as a future feature. How news should affect decisions is still undecided.
- 2026-11-26: Dislikes that the strategies do not look at social media or forums, including Twitter/X, LinkedIn, and Reddit. Wants these sources considered as a future feature; their role in decisions is still undecided.
- 2026-11-26: Dislikes that macroeconomic information, such as GDP, CPI, inflation, and Fed announcements, does not inform trading decisions. Wants macroeconomic inputs considered as a potential future feature.

- 2026-11-26: Dislikes that stock selection does not evaluate company fundamentals, financial health, valuation, or business quality. Wants this analysis added at some point as a future feature and improvement to the project. This should cover revenue and growth; gross, operating, and net margins; profit and earnings per share (EPS); valuation ratios such as price-to-earnings (P/E), forward P/E, and price-to-book (P/B); debt levels and debt-related ratios; cash flow; all three financial statements (income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement); industry conditions, competition and competitive position; and management and executive quality. The desired improvement is to understand the underlying businesses alongside price movements. Timing, data sources, and how these inputs affect decisions remain undecided.
- 2026-11-26: On sizing, wants to hold more of the stocks expected to rise more (for example, more NVIDIA than Tesla if NVIDIA is expected to do better) instead of the same amount of each. How to estimate which will rise more is still undecided.

These entries record preferences and future considerations, not implemented features or authorization to change trading behavior. Any future implementation must follow the project's paper-only, deterministic execution, risk, and validation rules.
